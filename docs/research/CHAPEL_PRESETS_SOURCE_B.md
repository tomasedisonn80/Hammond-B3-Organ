## What this review establishes, and what remains

Two readers independently corroborate the atlas's **approved chapel arrangement**.
The atlas attributes its own table to S019-01, a factory early-console service
manual compilation, printed Table IV page 4-1. Neither this comparison nor the
matching hashes independently verifies that underlying factory page. Its exact
edition, bytes and table must still be acquired and directly reviewed before
claiming that stronger source gate. The arrangement is not universal surviving
B-3 wiring, and no instrument specimen has been identified. [Atlas p122.]

The surrounding original pages distinguish nine fixed keys C#-A, adjustable-bank
keys A#/B and momentary contactless Cancel C. Ordinary selection preserves
inactive banks, held playing keys, generator state, the other manual and pedals.
Possible simultaneous latches require explicit contact routes and electrical
loading; summing independent preset outputs does not establish that behavior.
Upper B also changes percussion interlock, compensation and keying; re-entry
while keys remain held requires its own charge/contact history. [Atlas pp121-125.]

Next admit a separately identified, strictly validated matrix without changing
the existing 45 controls or saved-state contract. Require exact shape, key order,
digit strings, source hashes and independent-review provenance. Then design and
test a nominal exclusive selector and any explicit state migration. Factory
source comparison, specimen continuity, branch impedances, overlap timing,
loaded gains and acquired percussion transitions remain separate gates under
the [remaining M3 contract](../M3_REMAINING_AND_SCANNER_GATE.md). No runtime or
audio behavior was changed by this documentary import. [Atlas pp125-126.]
