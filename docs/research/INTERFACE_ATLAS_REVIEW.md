## Decision for the next phase

Advance M9 with the [native interface scope](../INTERFACE_CONTINUATION_SCOPE.md):
Play/Rotary/Service workspaces, usable shared live geometry, dimensional and flat
presentation, and a read-only inspection view. Pages 421-426 explicitly recommend
an evidence-only inspector before editor mutations or physical comparisons.
Pages 34, 379, 389, 414 and 425 require copied UI state and continuing audio
histories. Pages 431 and 437 support an equivalent flat control surface.

The known playable nominal engine, fixed-registration selectors, current 47
parameters and `B3CONTROLS4` remain the sound contract. The atlas's earlier
62-parameter proposal is historical source, not a replacement registry. Preset
selection, bank editing, Cancel, Panic and the whole saved-control state remain
different operations.

The reference's walnut, brass, charcoal, tablet depth and cabinet composition
guide appearance. Pages 409-413 and 431 require correct live labels and control
inventory even when generated art is wrong. The picture's STOP button does not
establish a stock brake, its percussion legends do not replace the verified
controls, and its key depiction does not replace 61+61 playing keys and 25 pedals.
Keep original concept art distinct from native executable screenshots and record
the remaining visual differences until an actual comparison passes.

Inspection can expose active nominal models, profile, copied registrations and
device status. It cannot fill missing physical measurements with invented volts,
RPM, angles, dimensions, specimen identity or zero-valued unknowns. Scanner,
AO-28, Leslie electronics, rotary acoustics, physical capture/calibration, native
accessibility and signed release retain their separate dependencies and gates.
