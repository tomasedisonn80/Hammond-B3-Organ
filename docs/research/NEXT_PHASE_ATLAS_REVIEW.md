## Whole plan

M0 identifies evidence and specimen/variant contracts; M1 supplies a bounded
framework-independent skeleton; M2 supplies shared continuous tone sources;
M3 covers contacts, drawbars, presets, pedals, percussion and expression; M4
models the scanner; M5 completes AO-28 electronics; M6 completes Leslie electrical
networks; M7 models rotary acoustics; M8 acquires/calibrates and validates held-out
physical evidence; M9 supplies native/plugin interfaces and qualified release.
The full [project plan](../PLAN.md) retains dependencies and milestone gates.
The atlas ten research waves, its M0–M9 implementation milestones and these ten
six-agent work waves are different organizations.
