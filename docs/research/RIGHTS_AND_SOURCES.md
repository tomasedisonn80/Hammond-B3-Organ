# Rights, provenance and source discovery

## Supplied atlas

The user-supplied `Hammond_B3_Leslie_Research_Atlas_500pp.pdf` was present in the
baseline repository (`501c68b`). Its evidence edition is 12 November 2026; it has
500 pages and 21,248,265 bytes. SHA-256:
`4ae13c3065c60d733d0daa33817259b3198cdb959070baecadc2981678e5e769`.
The [source manifest](source-manifest.json) and [review](ATLAS_REVIEW.md) preserve
this provenance. A matching hash proves byte identity, not ownership or a reuse
grant.

The supplier's authorship, underlying permissions and scope of redistribution
rights remain unresolved. These documentation additions grant no new broad
license to the supplied PDF, its images, quoted sources or linked materials.
Review and implementation provenance must remain distinct from redistribution
clearance. No license purchase or commercial clearance is recorded.

No additional third-party source code, manuals, photos, fonts, audio captures,
models or extracted PDF assets are bundled by this consolidation. The supplied
PDF remains the pre-existing research source. New project schemas and routing
tables are reconstructions with their own provenance, not recovered original
companions. Merely citing an implementation does not import its code or license.

## What is available and what is missing

The atlas's source/rights audit (pp. 475-480) describes 43 source visuals, 28 patent
plates, 40 Commons records, three publisher figures and 71 staged image paths.
Their original source catalog, individual asset manifests, permissions and
derivative files were absent from the baseline. These historical counts do not
establish that the assets are available or cleared here. The 88 photo-task
families on pp. 469-474 were unacquired; they are tasks, not a photo collection.

The complete PDF link metadata contains **1,982 link annotations and 239 unique
URL targets**. Directory pp. 493-499 each contain 28 distinct targets; p. 500 has
21. Repeated/wrapped rectangles are not independent sources, and different URLs
may point to the same document. Directory discovery is not a verified access,
authority, version, applicability or permission record.

## Evidence and reuse decisions

Access, technical authority, specimen applicability and reuse rights are separate
questions. For each acquired item, retain publisher/creator, exact edition or
commit, original and mirror URLs, acquisition date, local hash, printed/PDF page
and region, intended use, applicable terms, required notices and unresolved
permission questions (pp. 19-24, 355-359, 475-480).

For photographs and derived art, retain the original master, derivative ancestry,
crop/retouch/geometry changes, creator and source-specific license evidence.
P005/P020 are retouched and excluded from metrology; P012 removes/interpolates a
warning board; P016/P017 alter perspective/aspect; P028/P030 identities remain
unresolved. P035's creator is identified by the atlas as bbear66, distinct from
uploader BEG (pp. 475-480). These are inherited audit findings, not fresh checks
of those sites.

Patent expiration alone does not establish copyright clearance for every scan
or plate. The atlas's selected-plate observations do not authorize new material.
Likewise its dated VST3 3.8, JUCE 9 and pinned setBfree license accounts are
discovery leads; verify the exact selected revision's primary terms and notices
before adopting or distributing that dependency. This document does not select
a framework, dependency license or project-wide license (pp. 391, 403-408,
477-480).

Release preparation must inventory the actual distributed files and their
specific permissions/notices. Keep unintended scans, source trees and unrelated
assets out of staging, and retain the final dependency/asset manifest beside
the build and test identities (pp. 408, 456, 479-480, 485-486). Missing rights
records block the affected redistribution claim; they do not turn a known
technical reference into measured evidence or prevent independent scoped work.
