one independent engine, a VST3 plugin and a standalone instrument with the atlas's
dimensional/accessible interface. M0-M9 remains the implementation order in

## Decisions carried into the new contracts

- **Keep immutable observations separate from interpretations.** Hashes identify
  bytes; neither valid metadata nor a valid file authenticates a specimen or
  qualifies a measurement (pp. 15-18, 355-360, 423).
- **Preserve raw versus derived meaning.** Capture v1's digital declaration
  describes the raw acquisition. Crops, channel selection, resampling, gain and
  delay changes retain their own identities and transformations; their headers
  need not equal the original stream (pp. 356-359, 423).
- **Group related evidence before evaluation.** Trace complete parent graphs;
  keep complete takes, synchronized channels, sessions, declared gesture families
  and all derivatives together. Distinguish fitting, validation/model selection
  and final assessment through a companion contract (pp. 359, 366, 378, 484).
- **Freeze comparison choices.** Explicit windows, channels, units, floors,
  correction policies and thresholds precede evaluation. Report uncompensated
  and permitted compensated errors. No per-note normalization, arbitrary time
  warp, phase reset or residual EQ may silently improve a verdict (pp. 364, 423).
- **Retain fail and inconclusive.** Missing calibration, synchronization, reliable
  signal or resolvable uncertainty cannot produce a physical pass. The atlas
  supplies no universal fidelity threshold (pp. 347, 366, 484).
- **Keep upstream dependencies.** Dry-organ validation precedes cabinet fitting;
  internal parameters need appropriate intermediate observations. A small final
  residual does not establish wiring, loading or identifiability (pp. 251-252,
  361-366, 449, 490).

## What this reading does not provide

The source states that no new instrument measurements were acquired (p. 2).
It does not supply the missing original companion code/data or a physical corpus.
Its published third-party measurements, original simulations and proposed
thresholds remain different evidence classes. The session can qualify tooling
using explicit synthetic examples; actual acquisition, calibration, fitting,
listening, device/host and physical fidelity gates need their own observations.
