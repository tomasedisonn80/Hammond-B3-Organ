## Direct visual observations

Both inspected images are 1672 x 941. The native application preserves the broad
reference composition: central serif title, warm cheeks, top performance plate,

### Complementary percussion/performance surface

The native performance plate is visibly smoother and flatter than the reference.
The reference has darker, uneven warm-gray metal, fine visible grain, softer
local illumination and stronger recess/border cues around the percussion area.
The native plate has a more uniform vertical field with sparse fine marks. The
current white/gold tablets remain clearly readable; their outlines, dark shadows
and ivory faces already follow the reference's general direction.

The native percussion heading and flanking rules are more lightly spaced and
less strongly illuminated than the reference. A material-only slice should leave
those text arguments and geometry alone initially, so changed material can be
qualified independently from a subsequent typography or tablet-shading slice.
The lower eligibility text is additional, truthful live information and must stay
readable against any new surface.

Read-only source inspection found one specific complementary hook:
`paint_reference_instrument` calls `reference_panel` for
`layout.performance_panel` with the Original material. Upper and Lower racks
already have separate material selectors. The percussion heading, divider,
expression/pedal content and eligibility text are painted after that panel.
This offers a bounded future selector for the performance interior without
changing the separately owned H1 header or reworking musical controls. It is a
proposal for root's ownership/contract review, not authorization of a particular
field or a completed comparison.

### Artwork must not override the live meanings

The concept's four visible tablet faces read ON, SOFT, NORM and FAST while its
underlabels read PERC, VOL, DECAY and HARM. That would put **NORM under DECAY**
and **FAST under HARM**, contradicting p.418's documented independent choices:
enable, Normal/Soft, Fast/Slow and Second/Third. The current application reads
ON, NORM, SLOW and 2ND for the shown state, with the correct underlabels and a
REQUIRES UPPER B line. Copying the concept's generated words would introduce a
functional error. Pages 409 and 411 expressly separate art direction from
semantic text and engineering authority.

Other material and composition differences remain visible: native drawbar tracks
are more angular/flat, current manual keys have a denser repeated pattern because
the engineering inventory is retained, and the header utility buttons differ
from the reference's preset-name strip. The native expression/pedal section and
right-side operating controls deliberately represent implemented functions; the
concept instead depicts scanner and cabinet/microphone controls that cannot be
presented as connected audio. The nominal-dry title caption and not-connected
labels remain truthful. No app screenshot establishes physical fidelity.

**Literal whole-interface 1:1 remains unmet and unwaived.** A local performance
material improvement must report its exact scope and remaining differences; a
smaller material error does not prove whole-image parity.
