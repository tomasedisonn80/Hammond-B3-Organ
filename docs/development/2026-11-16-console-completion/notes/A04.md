## Project purpose recovered from this range

The project aims to reproduce a specifically identified B-3/AO-28 and vintage
Leslie system through explicit electrical, mechanical and acoustic boundaries.
The target is not defined by a generic rotary effect or a visually convincing
cabinet. These chapters repeatedly separate source-backed topology, numerical
agreement, measured specimen agreement and product execution. The atlas's
limited mathematical fixtures are described as earlier source results; they
are not new tests executed in this checkout.

Every parameter needs units, source status, physical configuration and a valid
domain. Missing values remain unknown, rather than acquiring zero or a convenient
modern value. The implementation order starts with electrical ports and offline
references, continues through distinct horn/drum trajectories and owned acoustic
paths, and only then fits microphone and room behavior. That ordering prevents
one downstream fit from concealing an incorrect upstream circuit.
