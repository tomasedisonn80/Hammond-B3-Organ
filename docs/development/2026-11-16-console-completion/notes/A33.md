  real post/disabled-gate outcome once. The hook table names ordinary dispatch,
  its sole recovery batch, direct MIDI notes/releases, CC11, stopped legacy
  staging, fresh observations and every close/restart/failure branch.

  ordinary post-Start complete snapshot and wait for its own original proof.
  Never manufacture default acknowledgement or reuse the pre-Start token. Tag
  startup/restart/stopped-Load staging so late proof cannot clear newer intent;
  failures remain unavailable until an explicit later valid intention.

  Save-availability projection. Existing public pending=false cannot identify a
  proved stopped copy, and browser-side receipt duplication would weaken the
  authority boundary. A copied availability flag remains advisory to each
  fresh authoritative request.

  keyboard/Space/pointer/MIDI owners and original Ups, gates Escape before its
  existing release action, retains physical suppression across closure and
  restores validated focus. Ordinary overlays retain their own policy.
- Bind actual storage to its one immutable effect, all five protected paths and
  a fresh final authorization; prohibit event/MIDI/replay pumping or reentrant
  observation side effects through commit/result. Distinguish SavedWithWarning,
  terminal Unconfirmed, supersession and actual recovery from optimistic names.
- Preserve independent legacy and named references, both scope meanings, stable
  selection/ambiguity/staleness, existing UI/music inventory and separate owners.
