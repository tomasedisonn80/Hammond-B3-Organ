relationship mapping; isolated successful shards cannot prove global split
independence. The future complete inventory or versioned extension remains a
specified engineering task, not an already implemented facility.

inspects raw audio during its full-corpus audit. The guide preserves the original
and requires separately qualified decoder support for that workflow; it does not
suggest demoting raw data to support files or inventing a compatible recording.

boundaries, Leslie loads, actual rotor/pose evidence and independent product gates
remain separate dependencies. The new plan introduces neither invasive hardware
