## Implemented behavior

The public interface is `ReplayError`,
`verify_report(kind, input_path, report_path)` and `main`. The explicit kind is
`job` or `study`; no saved field chooses an executable path or dynamic module.
A saved complete report is read once under confined, bounded regular-file checks,
strict JSON admission and the exact job/study envelope rules. Its original bytes
must equal canonical UTF-8 JSON exactly; reordered, compact, CRLF or otherwise
reformatted copies reject before execution. The original SHA/size is observed
from the actually parsed byte buffer.

The wrapper admits exact top-level keys, versions/contracts, embedded original
inputs, source/runtime envelopes, permanent unknown-claim fields, binding
relationships and outcome precedence. Study envelopes also require every
ordered nested job, common corpus/source/runtime identities, exact work sums
and status/role counts. This is strict envelope admission. Other bounded nested
corpus/metric contents remain complete comparison data; the wrapper does not
certify their truth from their presence or a declared status. It does not
reserialize an originally compact policy into the policy parser's 64 KiB bound.

Before calling a public runner, the wrapper reads/validates/hashes the actual
original input and requires its consumed byte identity and complete parsed
value to match the saved report's declared input. Exact canonical value
comparison avoids Python's Boolean/integer and integer/float equality shortcuts.
The public `run_job` or `run_study` then executes once with its complete existing
corpus/audio/policy/resource/snapshot behavior. No cached report substitutes for
execution, and no producer is monkey-patched or modified by the implementation.

The fresh complete report is admitted and canonically serialized. Its consumed
input, runtime and actual source observations must agree with wrapper preflight.
The entire original saved byte sequence is compared with the entire fresh
canonical byte sequence; equality is not decided from a status, selected metric,
stored SHA or caller Boolean. Summary hashes identify each complete sequence.
A matched failed or inconclusive comparison remains failed or inconclusive in
the explicit underlying engineering-status fields; replay itself reports match.

The summary preserves both source/runtime inventories and their equality flags.
Those differences are context only, not proof of corruption or historical truth.
All ten physical/provenance claim fields remain `not_established`. The verifier
adds its own actual regular source observation and current runtime, without
absolute paths, hostname, timestamps, elapsed time or a hidden full-report copy.

The wrapper retains saved-report, initial-input and associated-source snapshots
through its final return, with a defensive cap of twelve roots/leaves. Current
job/study arrangements need at most nine/ten. Their public runners retain their
own complete archive snapshots through their own return. The wrapper explicitly
does not extend every artifact snapshot past that boundary or promise atomic
preflight. Current source/input/report files must remain quiescent.

Reports are bounded at 16 MiB, 1,048,576 value nodes, depth 32/40 for job/study
and numeric magnitude 4e18. The compact summary is bounded at 64 KiB, 8,192
nodes, depth 12 and magnitude 4e18. Overflow rejects without truncation.
The CLI rejects unknown, abbreviated, duplicate, empty or incorrectly typed
arguments, writes canonical UTF-8/LF through binary streams independently of
terminal text settings, and optionally publishes one new summary before stdout
through the unchanged no-clobber helper. Existing destinations are preserved;
complete publication/cleanup and stdout-delivery failures retain their explicit
outcomes. Cancellation propagates. Match exits 0, mismatch/rejection exits 1,
and usage exits 2; there is no replay exit 3.
