   enumeration's eighteen LC sections and nineteen output voltages over the

   from output count.
2. **Root algebra:** p. 177 identifies printed equation 7 as inconsistent with
   general elimination and conflicting source signs in equations 13/15.
   With the page's convention, M = I - Phi*S11; multiplication order is material.
   The 100 synthetic systems and 1.11e-16 result on p. 180 are the atlas's
   narrow algebra result, not a new result from this worker or a full circuit pass.
3. **WDF leaves/switches:** p. 180 flags equation 19's incorrect inductor DC
   limit, equation 23's failed positive-inductance adaptation and equation 14's
   open/short labels. The declared convention gives capacitor +delay,
   inductor -delay, open b=a and short b=-a (p. 176).
4. **Prewarp units:** p. 178 identifies the Hz/radian inconsistency. At 44,100
   samples/s, 7,075 Hz gives approximately 24.8133 microseconds effective period;
   treating 7,075 as radians/s gives approximately 22.7245 microseconds. Neither
   is a calibrated hardware choice.
5. **AO-28 roster:** p. 195 reconciles theory paragraph 5-46 with Figure 24:
   V3 already is the 12BH7. An extra output socket would be an invented part.
6. **C56/C59 conflict:** p. 217 raises the redraw collision; p. 239 says the
   factory inset identifies the later 10/350 capacitor as C59. Retain C59 for
   that namespace and retain the conflicting redraw observation, with actual
   specimen applicability unresolved.
7. **Catalog/glyph conflicts:** pp. 236 and 239 preserve the duplicated
   6-6-43 callout, ambiguous item-47 resistance, unresolved primary R62 glyph,
   secondary-only expression-capacitance annotation and alternate C32 values.
   These are separate issues, not permission to merge convenient readings.
8. **Units and method semantics:** p. 237's vintage MF corroborates microfarads;
   SPICE M/m means milli, while meg means one million. P. 206 separates triode
