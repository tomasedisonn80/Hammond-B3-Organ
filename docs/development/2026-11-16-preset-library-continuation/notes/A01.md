### Console behavior and modern registration files have different roles

The documented inventory is two 61-key manuals and a 25-note pedalboard
(p. 13), nine playing contacts per manual key, hence **549 per manual / 1,098
across both manuals**, with preset contacts counted separately (pp. 37, 42).
The console has four independent nine-drawbar manual banks and two pedal
drawbars: **38 retained settings** (p. 49). Footage and semantic identity must
remain stable across layout changes; a nine-digit registration rejects missing
or out-of-range digits rather than repairing them silently.

Page 50 defines vintage selector behavior: C cancels, C-sharp through A select
nine wired registrations, A-sharp/B select left/right banks, and upper B permits
percussion when enabled. Fixed registration vectors, bank positions and selected
routes are separate. Editing an inactive bank must not change the active wired
or other-bank registration. Preset keys are routing controls, never sounding
notes or oscillator creation commands. Modern total-state snapshots belong to
a separately named software layer (pp. 50, 54).

The four percussion tablets have distinct on/off, harmonic, level and decay
meanings. Global Normal/Soft is separate from percussion Normal/Soft. One scanner
selector serves separate Upper/Lower routing; Great vibrato also applies to
pedals in the cited topology (p. 51). Expression position survives a registration
change. UI/camera visibility and animation cannot create a second parameter
system or determine audio timing (pp. 34, 39–42, 51–54).

The printed 0–8 drawbar scale is not evidence for mechanically isolated detents
or a linear gain law. The cited continuous-contact mechanism joins two contacts
through a 1-ohm resistor; an initial nine-gain model is explicitly approximate,
and actual overlap/loading needs measurements (p. 52). Page 54 proposes held-chord
selector changes, inactive-bank editing, independent soft controls, coherent
restoration and no-gap tests; it does not report physical execution of them.

### Source, filter, leakage and downstream boundaries must be explicit

Pages 67–72 distinguish geometry, flux linkage, induced voltage and loaded
terminal voltage. A post-filter capture cannot uniquely identify raw pickup
properties. Normalized amplitude is not calibrated volts. A whole-turn
representation may retain eccentricity that a single-tooth waveform cannot.
For physical coefficients, differentiating flux linkage supplies the sign,
phase and speed factor; an arbitrary sampled waveform is not the same model.
Bandlimit retained harmonics and modulation bandwidth before evaluating sources.

The earlier generator-filter grouping is 1–43 resistance-wire shunt, 44–48
reactor without series tuning capacitor, 49–54 reactor plus 0.255 microfarad,
and 55–91 reactor plus 0.105 microfarad (p. 73). Historical `mf/mfd` means
microfarads here: these are `2.55e-7 F` and `1.05e-7 F`, not millifarads.
Bulletin 16's revised 37–48 network and applicability must be represented
separately: 50 microfarad/3 V capacitor, 10 ohm/half-watt resistor, B-3 starting
serial 93990, with possible retrofits (p. 74). The illustrative 318.31 Hz RC
corner is not a measured assembled response.

Preserve signed winding coupling and passivity, measured complex phase, port
loading and revision. A fixed-load periodic LTI response may be compiled into
complex coefficients, but this does not establish startup or changing-load
equivalence. Apply a measured filter only once; ordinary notes do not reset its
state (pp. 75–78). The proposed 0.1 dB/1-degree numerical target on p. 78 is an
engineering proposal, not a factory tolerance or achieved pass.

Leakage is a located, source-correlated transfer, distinct from noise, hum,
mechanical sound and separately evidenced post-contact feedthrough (pp. 79–84).
Adjacency is physical/electrical, not necessarily semitone adjacency. The
gain-only sparse model is an empirical feedforward approximation; edge polarity,
boundary and units remain explicit. Leakage amount scales off-diagonal terms;
zero preserves the diagonal path, and coherent sums must not be normalized away
(pp. 81, 84). Sparse/dense agreement, single-edge locality, invalid-profile
rejection and held-out musical tests are software gates. They cannot manufacture
the absent measured 91-source coupling matrix.
