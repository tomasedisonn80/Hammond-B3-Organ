### Specific AO-28 constraints that remain open

The eight-socket roster on p. 193 is V1/V2 6AU6, V3 12BH7, V4 12AX7, V5/V6 6C4,
V7 12AU7 and V8 6X4. V3A drives the scanner boundary and V3B drives T3; the
inconsistent prose cited on p. 195 must not create a ninth socket. This is
documentary context from the atlas, not a new independent factory transcription.

The source explicitly reports no measured reference preamp on p. 197, no complete
AO-28 nonlinear implementation on p. 216, and no whole-AO-28 numerical or physical
qualification on p. 240. The primary acquisition gaps include:

- Identified AO-28 chassis code, mains variant, installed devices and actual

  (pp. 196, 236, 239-240).
- Complete, independently traced terminal connectivity, active values and source
  conflicts. The factory C59/redraw C56 collision and duplicated catalog callout

  output-leg polarities, operating points and volts-per-sample scaling
  (pp. 199-204, 211).

  organ distortion from downstream Leslie and room contributions

## Permissible independent M7 foundation

**Recommendation:** continue a bounded, named nominal rotary mechanics and
acoustic-propagation research module while the other sessions own scanner and
standalone development. Its input should be declared horn and drum excitations
with explicit units or an explicitly normalized research convention. It can
verify motion, event timing, causal propagation and interpolation without
asserting a complete AO-28 or Leslie electrical chain.

This recommendation follows the ownership and separate-qualification contracts
on pp. 241-247 and the current repository plan. The detailed physical rotary
mechanism and geometry in later chapters belong to the next reader's source
review; this assignment does not supply their nominal speeds or dimensions.

The new module should own rotor motion, geometry and its chosen propagation
operator exactly once. It should explicitly exclude any electrical crossover,
driver, cabinet, microphone or room response not implemented and qualified under
that module's declared boundary. If a later directional capture contains one of
those responses, its ownership must be reconciled before connecting it. The
same rule applies to source material already containing delay or room sound.

No fitted rotary EQ, artificial saturation or loudness adjustment should be used
to absorb unresolved dry-organ, scanner or AO-28 discrepancies. A synthetic
audition can exercise a proposed subsystem, but it is neither a direct G-G
recording nor a measured Leslie response. Keep the research path separate from
the default Engine and native application's qualified dry output until explicit
integration, parameter/state migration and compatibility acceptance exist.
