### Binding rotary state, timing and geometry consequences

1. **Commands are not state resets.** Chorale/Tremolo changes targets while
   horn and drum retain separate histories (p. 419). Select a rising/falling
   ramp from `target - current speed`, not merely the switch label (p. 466).
   The historical 5-8-second bass adjustment is not universal tau or horn
   timing. A historical brake accessory is optional, not a baseline stop law.
2. **Each microphone solves its own emission time.** Page 467 gives
   `t = e + |m - q(e)| / c` and
   `de/dt = 1 / (1 - u dot v / c)`, with `u` from emission position toward
   the microphone. All paths query the same physical rotor trajectory at
   their respective historical emission times (pp. 449, 460, 467).
3. **Preserve endpoint and coordinate signs.** The page-467 linear formula is
   `(1-mu)*x[i] + mu*x[i+1]`, and the declared circular norm contains
   `-2*R*a*cos(theta)`. Test those independently before using optimized
   interpolation or path calculations. The cited 2002 experiment concerns
   a Leslie 600 horn, not a selected 122 or bass calibration.
4. **One effect has one owner.** Propagation, directional radiation, fixed
   cabinet scattering, gain, microphone and room responses cannot be applied
   twice (pp. 449, 452, 460, 489). Whole-cabinet angular responses cannot
   generally be rotated by a simple microphone-azimuth offset (p. 467).
5. **Time-varying operators need time-varying tests.** Input-time and
   observation-time angular weights are different operators; matching frozen
   angles does not validate their order (pp. 467, 483). Retain historical
   trajectory/filter context across commands and explicit profile transitions.
6. **Display and acoustics have separate clocks.** Reduced motion, camera
   movement, editor absence, dropped diagnostic snapshots and rendering
   failure cannot alter rotor time (pp. 425, 428, 431, 437, 442, 485).
   Acoustic microphone geometry is not the UI camera transform (p. 431).
7. **Coordinates and gain stay inspectable.** Mic positions use metres and a
   declared frame. Geometry is admitted before publication; moving a mic
   cannot reset phase. Do not hide automatic level matching inside distance
   editing (p. 419). Apply the Z-up/glTF conversion once if visual assets are
   later introduced, including pivots and orientations (pp. 428, 468).
