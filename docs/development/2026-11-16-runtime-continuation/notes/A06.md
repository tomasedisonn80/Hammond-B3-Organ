- **Resolve contact history before summing.** A folded route shares the same
  oscillator, while its contact trajectory remains distinct. A note never restarts

- **Retain physical and routed state separately.** Cancel changes preset routing;
  it does not manufacture note-offs. Suppression of upper-B 1-foot audio does not
