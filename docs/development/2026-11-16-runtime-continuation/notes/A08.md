## Main observed differences and design choices

The reproducible report retains 72 fixed quiet/shoulder rectangles, grouped by
Upper/Lower, ivory/ebony and six material bands. Each group has exact RGB sums,
encoded-channel Y′ numerator/denominator, pixel identity and historical actual-app
observations on three idle representative keys. Masks were selected from the
original image before any key candidate exists. Normalized material coordinates
avoid pretending that the concept and the correct 61-key live manual have matching
key counts or calibrated physical dimensions.

The useful changes are concrete:

- Upper ivory front high/low Y′ is **118.166/107.519** in the source versus
  **147.953/120.469** in the historical app. Lower is **107.340/93.728** versus
  **148.000/120.298**. The chosen two-band fronts darken the body while reducing
  the excessive within-front ramp. A narrow top shoulder remains separate.
- Upper ebony back/middle/nose is **19.042/51.903/63.970** versus
  **51.124/39.326/26.184**; Lower is **17.215/51.925/63.499** versus
  **49.800/38.727/25.903**. The chosen inset bands reverse the old decreasing
  ramp into the source's forward sheen; the front remains dark.
- Ivory top changes are small except for the deeper Lower rear shadow. Side
  highlights/shadows use the same existing shapes at lower alpha. Ebony's warm
  inner border becomes quieter and the front glint becomes neutral and narrow.

The source image is an uncalibrated generated concept. Its encoded RGB statistics
are not physical reflectance/luminance and its shoulder variations are not a
specimen measurement. Colors remain explicit artistic choices. No one combined
similarity score or literal whole-interface equality is claimed.
