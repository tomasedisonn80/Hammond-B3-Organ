## Requirements most relevant to the next M3 work

### Motion, contact events and audio are separate responsibilities

Each of 122 manual playing keys has nine contacts: **549 per manual**, with
preset contacts counted separately (p. 37). The model must retain per-contact
thresholds and independent states, not infer one universal closure ordering
from a drawing. The first kinematic approximation is (p. 39):

```text
p(t) = max(0, r * x(t) - g)
close j when p(t) >= theta_on[j]
release j when p(t) <= theta_off[j]
```

Here displacement/clearance/thresholds have stated length units, and `r` is
dimensionless. Independent press/release thresholds permit hysteresis. A MIDI
note only initiates a declared synthetic trajectory; it cannot reconstruct
unobserved finger position. Velocity-dependent transient behavior must not
silently scale sustained drawbar loudness (p. 39).

Page 40 specifically requires fractional crossing timestamps for the selected
band-limited switching method, explicit release evolution, and repressing from
an incompletely returned key. Source identity, contact state and onward preset
routing remain separate. It forbids using animation as the audio clock and
rejects adding arbitrary noise to conceal incorrect switching. Identical timed
inputs must produce equivalent contacts across host partitions and GUI rates.

The useful physical acquisitions are synchronized position/contact/dry-audio
records with clock alignment, repeatability, partial presses, repeated strokes,
release-only motion and held-out keys/gestures (pp. 41–42). A published 1967 C-3's
approximately 9 mm front travel, 4 mm felt travel and 2 mm clearance are
specimen-specific examples, **not B-3 calibration constants** (pp. 38, 42).

### Continuous shared sources are an invariant

The source registry separates tone ID 1–91, array index 0–90, physical terminal,
shaft/tooth identity and MIDI reference (pp. 43, 61). Two routes selecting one
source must read the same phase state (p. 26). Note events, cancelled presets,
silence, disconnected output, buffer changes and editor behavior do not restart
oscillators or source filters (pp. 40, 42, 46, 63, 76).

Relevant identities and checks are:

```text
f_wheel = (rpm_motor / 60) * gear_ratio * tooth_count          [p. 26]
phi_i = tooth_count_i * shaft_angle_j + pickup_offset_i       [pp. 45, 63]
phi_i[n+1] = wrap(phi_i[n] + 2*pi*f_i[n]/Fs)                 [p. 63]
f_ET(w) = 440 * 2^((w-46)/12), MIDI = w + 23                 [p. 66]
```

The ET anchors are tone 1/MIDI 24 (~32.703196 Hz), tone 46/MIDI 69 (440 Hz),
tone 91/MIDI 114 (~5919.910763 Hz). They identify a reference calculation;
production gearing and measured frequencies are distinct profiles. Page 62's
rational ratios are a labeled setBfree comparison, not verified factory gear
inventory. Unknown initial phase offsets may be reproducibly seeded but remain
modeled (p. 63).

Source signals are evaluated once per sample and reused by all routes (pp. 64,
70, 82). Leakage is a separately identified feedforward transfer before or
after contacts as evidenced; a terminal capture may already contain it (pp.
79–83). Coherent sums must not receive automatic normalization (p. 81). These
requirements argue for evaluating causal audio against absolute **input time**
and sharing each source value across contact routes, independently of output
latency and callback partitioning.

### Control semantics must survive research integration

The physical surface retains four nine-drawbar manual banks plus two pedal
settings, all independent (p. 49). Stable IDs include manual, bank and footage.
The normal preset rule selects Cancel, nine wired registrations, A#/left bank
or B/right bank per manual; upper B is percussion eligible (pp. 50–51). Page
50 also documents a possible physical multi-latch behavior and points to the
later chapter; it does not authorize silently changing the repository's current
single-selector/state contract.

Editing an inactive bank must leave the sounding registration unchanged.
Cancellation changes onward routing without creating a sounding preset note or
resetting a source (pp. 50, 52, 54). Global Soft and percussion Soft are distinct;
Great scanner routing includes pedals in the documented topology (p. 51), but
that documentary rule cannot authorize wiring an unqualified scanner into the
current dry Engine.

Continuous-contact drawbars use a two-contact/resistor arrangement. Nine marked
positions or a smooth amplitude interpolation are declared approximations,
not an already calibrated electrical overlap law (p. 52).
