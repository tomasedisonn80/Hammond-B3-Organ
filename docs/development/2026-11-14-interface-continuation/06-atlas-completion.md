## Dependency-ready M9 interface slice

**Implement Play/Rotary/Service navigation and a read-only evidence inspector
against copied application state.** This is expressly supported by the order on
pp. 421-426 and can proceed before missing physical calibration. It should retain
the current **47 sound parameters**, all existing identities and meanings,
`B3CONTROLS4` and its legacy readers, and the current nominal core. The atlas's
62-entry proposed registry (pp. 452, 468, 482) must not overwrite the actual
exported contract.

1. **Play:** preserve live musical controls and expose the difference between
   edited bank, sounding route, pending/applied state and percussion eligibility.
   Page 418 specifically forbids moving a stored 1-foot drawbar to zero merely to
   illustrate an audio interlock. Do not invent physical decay or charge readings.
2. **Rotary:** show the exact decorative cabinet and a clear status that its
   downstream audio path is not connected. Inspection selection is view state.
   Page 419 separates selected microphone, commanded speed and independent rotor
   histories; until those algorithms are connected, do not imply functional
   acoustic edits through decorative controls. Baseline two-speed behavior and
   the optional historical brake/accessory profile remain distinct.
3. **Service:** expose the actual active nominal profile/model identity,
   implemented signal path, applied registration/control values and factual
   device status. Documentary, derived/proposed and unknown information must be
   legible independently of color. Unknown specimen, calibration, voltage or
   geometry values remain unknown with their reason and next acquisition
   (pp. 421-423). A parts inventory is not a complete circuit.
4. **Presentation:** use the reference's wood/charcoal/brass appearance with
   semantic, live legends. Page 431 explicitly retains correct controls even
   when concept artwork is wrong. Sculpting, keyboard depth and camera changes
   must not obscure labels or shrink usable hit targets (pp. 420, 430, 435-436).
   A shared geometry transform is required for actual perspective changes.
5. **Lifecycle:** tabs, assembly selection, flat/visual mode, resize and reduced
   motion emit no sound-parameter changes and do not reset phase, notes or
   percussion history. Close an original drag before rebinding. Key-up delivery
   must still release its captured owner after a view change (pp. 420, 434, 442).
   Deactivation/close releases UI owners without releasing external MIDI owners.

Read-only information is the bounded first deliverable. Draft-profile editing,
waveform/spectrum plots and physical overlays have additional contracts and are
not implied by a polished inspector. Page 425 requires timestamped/identified
bounded snapshots, explicit stale/gap states and off-callback formatting,
analysis and resource work. A UI frame stall cannot block audio or lose musical
events. Camera coordinates cannot silently become microphone coordinates.
