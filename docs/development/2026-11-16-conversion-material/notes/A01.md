## Objective and evidence boundaries

identified AO-28 revision and Leslie 122. Leslie 147 and modern instruments have
separate profiles. The atlas acquired no physical measurements and supplies no
completed engine or demonstrated 1:1 result (p. 2).

Implementation order is reference/data contracts; testable skeleton; shared
sources; console; scanner; AO-28; Leslie electronics; rotary acoustics;
calibration; host/product release (p. 5). Its research waves are not milestone
completion or this session's staffing. Functional, specimen/audio, visual and

## M3 converter recommendation

Pages 39–42 require independent hysteresis, partial closure, fractional timestamps,
release/repress history and block-invariant events. Pages 63–66 require continuous
shared phase through notes and silence. Consequently, freeze exact contact-edge
identity/direction/equality/command correspondence; scalar rounding bounds alone
cannot certify switching behavior. A target rounded across a threshold changes a
musical state even with tiny numeric error.

Use the current plan's proposal and review to admit rounding, collisions,
position/root budgets, strict outputs and atomic failure before implementation.
Keep the first converter offline and trajectory-only, preserving original grid
and fractional identities. Acceptance does not establish waveform accuracy,
physical fidelity, indefinite capacity or live callback suitability.

Missing evidence includes synchronized per-key travel/contact/dry-audio captures,
clock uncertainty, bounce, loaded resistance and specimen geometry (pp. 38–42).
The approximately 9 mm front travel is one 1967 C-3 observation, not a universal
B-3 specification.
