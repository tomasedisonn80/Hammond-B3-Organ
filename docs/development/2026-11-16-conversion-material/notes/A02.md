## Findings that bind the next M3 converter

1. **Source sharing must preserve distinct contact events.** Pages 97–102 separate
   playing key, electrical tone ID, physical terminal and zero-based array index.
   At top manual key 61, drawbars 2/5/8 share tone 80 and 4/6/9 share tone 85.
   Page 100 requires independent contact timing and branch contribution before
   optional linear aggregation. Neither conversion nor event certification may
   deduplicate contacts because they share a source or contribute zero gain.
   Mapping identity is independent of sample rate, while waveform and switching
   bandwidth are not (pp. 100, 107).

2. **A contact trajectory is not yet switched audio.** Page 116 derives the value
   jump from source amplitude at the actual edge; a source zero crossing can remove
   the value jump while leaving a slope jump. Pages 117–118 require fractional
   edge time, stable tied ordering, persistent histories and switching before
   bandwidth reduction. A small scalar time error alone therefore does not certify
   an audio error tolerance. Preserve a trajectory-only certificate boundary and
   leave audio reconstruction, phase evaluation, delay and tails to their own
   admitted contract.

3. **Retain nine independent hysteretic histories and their common motion cause.**
   Page 117 assigns each key nine connection histories and separates make from
   break. Partial travel can leave only a subset closed. Release is not guaranteed
   to be reversed attack. Ordinary MIDI supplies a named synthetic gesture, with
   velocity available for transient selection but no sustained-volume scaling.
   Converter rejection must not replace distinct events with one per-key event,
   collapse threshold differences or invent a shared press/release speed.

4. **Exact event topology matters even when output is silent.** Page 128 says that
   the physical eighth-harmonic keying contact is the 1-foot contact, not the eighth
   left-to-right drawbar; its state must survive a muted audible drawbar. Pages
   129–130 require elapsed-time recharge, zero recovery for a zero-duration opening,
   and stable off/on/preset order. Certification must include muted histories,
   occurrence counts and edge-versus-command equality, rather than grading only
   final bits, audible routes or a visually sorted list.

5. **No callback partition may redefine time.** Pages 106–108, 117–120 and 130
   require sample-time state, preserved history and equivalent traces under different
   partitions. Whole-epoch quantization followed by partitioning is safer than
   independently rounding local block coordinates. The native grid's own admitted
   tie and closure semantics remain authoritative; the atlas does not prescribe
   its integer tick grid or authorize silently changing it.

6. **The atlas supplies no ready quantization error budget.** Page 120 proposes
   normalized RMS error at most `1e-4` against a converged high-rate audio reference
   after gain/latency conventions are fixed. It explicitly calls this an engineering
   target, not a Hammond specification or executed result. It is not a threshold
   for position, root time or event-topology conversion. The next owner must freeze
   those separate units, budgets and acceptance/refusal predicates before evaluating
   candidate conversion results.

7. **A conversion artifact is neither a patch migration nor a runtime checkpoint.**
   Pages 126 and 155 distinguish saved controls from running phases, pending edges,
   contact state and filter histories. Keep the 47 current parameter identities,
   `B3CONTROLS4` and historical readers unchanged. A converter output can carry
   source/input identities and exact event evidence without authorizing live
   Engine, VST3 or standalone state publication.

8. **Strict admission and atomic failure are architectural requirements.** Pages
   92, 94 and 96 require explicit units, finite input, distinct unknown/zero values,
   duplicate-name rejection, semantic identity checks after structural validation,
   and preservation of the prior valid playback state on invalid loads. A new
   offline converter should similarly preflight bytes and paths, reject malformed
   inputs with a bounded specific diagnostic and avoid partially publishing a
   certificate/transport set. A successful schema check proves representation,
   not fidelity.

## Other engineering constraints retained

- The nominal drawbar law is `g(0)=0`, `g(p)=10^(3*(p-8)/20)` for p=1–8;
  it is explicitly provisional, not measured production winding data (pp. 109–114).
  Coherent same-source paths add amplitudes; two equal paths ideally add about
  6.0206 dB. Polyphony normalization must not hide a route/gain error (p. 111).
- Reverse C cancels the selected manual's latch and returns; it preserves held
  playing keys, generator phase, other manual and pedal state (p. 121).
  A#/B expose retained bank positions without copying them. The atlas Table IV
  on p. 122 is a named chapel matrix with a single-transcription evidence label,
  not a universal specimen registration; this fresh reading is not a replacement
  primary-document transcription review.
- Percussion is one manual-wide state, enabled through upper B and actual keying
  contact routing, with no new source oscillator and no note-local reset
  (pp. 124, 127–138). RC products on p. 135 are electrical calculations, not audible
  decay or measured recharge constants.
- Pedals require their own 25×8 map and four-bus mixture. The exceptional third
  contact repeats second-harmonic sources for rows 1–7, is disconnected for 8–12,
  and uses the higher source for 13–25 (pp. 139–144). Manual nine-contact conversion
  must not be generalized to pedals by silently padding/deduplicating routes.
- Expression controller calibration, physical expression transfer and downstream
  monitoring gain are distinct. An animated pedal cannot identify rotor capacitance,
  loading or AO-28 response (pp. 145–150).
- Scanner line and rotor work belong to their separate owner/gates. Pages 157–162
  distinguish 18 inductors, 18 capacitors and 19 observed voltages; pp. 163–168
  distinguish those from 16 physical stators and nine terminals. The p. 166 printed
  pseudocode contains **15** order entries although the prose/modulus require 16;
  visual inspection confirms this is printed, not extraction loss. Preserve the
  existing erratum and its source-specific resolution instead of copying the
  faulty array or altering scanner work during converter implementation.

## Missing evidence and release limits

Physical contact thresholds, complete make/break and bounce behavior, bare-contact
resistance, synchronization uncertainty and measured dry output are not supplied by
this range. The cited contact study is one 1967 C-3, not a universal B-3 calibration
(pp. 115, 117, 119). A future acquisition needs synchronized key position, all nine
contacts, source phase and dry electrical output, with independent held-out keys
and gestures (p. 119). Transformer loading, pedal reactor/transfer, expression
capacitance and AO-28 circuit gains remain additional measured dependencies.

Neither this reading nor a converter pass closes physical M3, audio reconstruction,
indefinite/full-organ streaming, real-time deadline, host/device or literal whole-UI
1:1 gates. Pages 85–168 contain hardware/control obligations but do not supply the
atlas's later art-direction geometry; the Lower material owner must use the
independently assigned visual-reference chapters and actual application captures.
