## Owned implementation and independent method

and the detailed [comparison review](../../../reviews/FOOTAGE_TYPOGRAPHY_COMPARISON.md).

independently. It derives old and new glyph-copy rectangles from the pinned
atlas and contract arithmetic, including binary32 operation boundaries, actual
Drawing baseline reconstruction, bearings, transparent gutters, SDL integer
destination extents, renderer scales, viewport and caller clip.

Only the old/new glyph union plus at most one device pixel is allowed. No
candidate helper or changed-pixel-derived mask is imported, and no complete
label box or old material allowance is reused. The 110-case mask was saved
before native execution. It covers 286,098,649 output pixels; canonical support
contains 7,928 pixels. Requested/reconstructed-baseline device boxes coincide
for all 9,548 declared glyph records.

The tool validates source/protocol/baseline/build/binary identities, independent
metadata/state/geometry, complete pair metadata equality, every protected RGBA
channel, cyan focus equality, literal native invariants, descriptive per-label
ink bounds/counts and twelve exact retained material patches/statistics. Its
80 MiB output cap and new-directory requirement prevent overwriting evidence.
Successful raw RGBA streams are transient; failed pairs would retain lossless
before/after and channel-difference images. All full comparison pairs passed.
