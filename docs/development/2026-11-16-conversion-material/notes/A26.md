## Contract decisions

The proposal uses a separate versioned manifest, compressed payload and compiler,
keeping the existing three assets and compiler unchanged. Ordinary compilation
uses bounded standard-library verification/expansion and a generated header
that reuses the unchanged `RgbImage` type. One optional startup texture and one
active-frame copy replace only the existing rim call; all preparation/copy
failures retain the exact procedural fallback. No new control, font, layout,
audio route or per-frame decoder/upload is introduced.

The critical source finding is that later rack shadows end at canonical y515,
upper-manual shadows begin at y525, and the cabinet's left shadow begins at
x1121. Consequently exact raw transport, exact unoccluded final rows and final
composited boundary pixels are distinct claims. The contract retains later
shadow order, reports every full-band source difference, and requires independent
compositing reconstruction and full-frame seam/double-darkness review. It does
not promise final equality for all 18 rows or erase a shadow to obtain it.

The finite proposal enumerates 32 supplemental size/scale/clip/state cases,
retains all 66 existing percussion/layout comparisons and the current material/
typography fixtures, and requires a genuine then-current before executable.
The mask is derived from old procedural primitives plus the new SDL copy,
with at most one device pixel of envelope and exact exterior/foreground/state
preservation. No whole-panel mask or changed-pixel-derived permission appears.
An independent pre-edit isolated-copy preflight must establish actual SDL
linear-sampling clip/full-companion agreement; source assumptions alone cannot
admit that behavior. Preparation/copy fault injection remains test-only.
