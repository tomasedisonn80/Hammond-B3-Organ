## Requirements recovered from the source

**Scope and evidence (pp. 2–24).** The baseline is a vintage B-3 with identified
AO-28 revision and Leslie 122. Leslie 147, 50-Hz hardware, modern B-3 and modified
specimens remain separate profiles. The atlas acquired no physical measurements.
Functional, specimen/audio and visual correspondence need independent evidence
(p. 13). Every physical value retains units, source/revision, applicability,
uncertainty and evidence class. Unknown remains null. A profile change must not
silently rewrite a previous qualification. M0–M9 implementation milestones are
distinct from ten research waves and this session's worker count.

**Sources and appearance (pp. 19–36, 53–60).** A clean redraw or patent is not an
independent production witness. Preserve original and annotated assets, page
locators, creator/rights records and applicability. P005 and P020 are retouched
and excluded from dimensional evidence. Cover art is an interface proposal;
verified semantic tables govern controls. Geometry should be checked with
calibrated landmarks and retained residuals, not an unsupported authenticity
percentage. Rendering consumes read-only state; hiding furniture or changing
presentation cannot change sound or serialized controls (p. 34).

**Console behavior (pp. 37–54).** Both 61-key manuals require nine contacts per
key, with separate preset contacts. A MIDI event starts a labeled surrogate
trajectory, not measured finger motion. Four nine-drawbar banks plus two pedal
drawbars retain independent values. C cancels; C#–A are wired registrations;
A#/B select independent banks; only enabled Upper B admits percussion. Inactive
bank edits leave the selected route unchanged. Great vibrato includes pedals in
the documented topology. Continuous-contact hardware does not establish a
linear slider gain law. Physical multi-latch behavior remains distinct from the
proposed exclusive selector. Audio timestamps, phase and release ownership must
survive presentation changes.

**Generator and electrical boundaries (pp. 43–48, 61–84).** Keep tone ID,
array index, MIDI note, terminal, pickup, shaft and compartment distinct. There
are 91 active sources and five balance-only disk positions; two routes selecting
one source share phase. Sources continue through silence and note changes.
Tone 1/MIDI 24, tone 46/MIDI 69 and tone 91/MIDI 114 are reference anchors.
The 12-TET formula and setBfree-derived rational table are separate models;
neither supplies a verified production gear inventory. The upper seven
192-tooth outputs require their own rule. Pickup voltage, loaded terminal
voltage, filter response and leakage injection points must prevent duplicate
filtering or coupling. Leakage follows evidenced physical/electrical adjacency,
not semitone neighbors. No measured coupling matrix is supplied. The 1965
filter revision is separate from the earlier network; serial inference alone
cannot prove installation. `mf/mfd` here means microfarads, not millifarads.
