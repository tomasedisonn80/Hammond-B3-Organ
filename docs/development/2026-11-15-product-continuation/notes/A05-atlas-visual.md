## Concrete next visual slice

The source art establishes atmosphere. **Page 409 explicitly excludes its key
counts and legends from engineering authority.** Page 411 places marked 0 near
the upper edge and 8 toward the player. Do not reverse the working drawbars to
imitate the picture's scale. Pages 413/419 also exclude an unconditional Stop
button; unavailable scanner/rotary functions must not become fake controls.

The v5 title, cabinet placement and manual depth already approach the reference.
Its smaller legends, short caps and regular key lighting remain visible gaps.
The next contract should authorize these **wide Dimensional-only** proposals:

1. **Legible footage and values.** Fit manual footage lettering toward 16-17
   logical pixels instead of the current 13, within existing label rectangles.
   Use measured fit or compact stacked fractions for long labels; preserve
   correct `1 3/5` footage. Increase numeric readout only within its existing
   16-pixel row. Never enlarge text past neighboring columns.
2. **Projected cap proportions.** Review 32-34-pixel manual caps against the
   present 26-pixel caps, retaining current width, brown/ivory/black grouping,
   upper-left light and a distinct lower front. Keep every painted extension
   inside the control surface and separate from labels/readouts at values 0/8.
   Adapt the ornament to available space; do not alter drag travel or hit bounds.
3. **Key-edge lighting.** Add a restrained warm side highlight and narrow dark
   counterpart inside each existing projected quad. Moderate the broad dark
   rear-to-bright-front ramp; preserve the separate darker front and rear recess.
   Correct full 61-key manuals naturally differ from the concept's inventory.
4. **Registration readability.** Make EDIT and applied SOUNDING state easier to
   read in the rack header. A proposed two-line title/status treatment can use
   the existing 32-pixel header allowance only if measured glyphs fit; otherwise
   retain the layout and improve contrast/fit. Never relabel desired state as
   applied or hide pending/focus/recovery information.

These are proposed targets, not measured improvements. Preserve all 68 controls,
24 presets, 147 playing keys, 47 parameters, state4/readers 1-3, shared hit/paint
geometry and held-owner release behavior. Keep Flat and compact frames unchanged.
