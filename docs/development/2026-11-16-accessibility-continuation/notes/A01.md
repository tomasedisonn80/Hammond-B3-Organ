## What the whole product is intended to become

Pages 2-5 define an identified vintage electromechanical B-3/AO-28 and a vintage
Leslie 122 as the reference configuration. A 147 needs a separate electrical
profile. The PDF explicitly reports no newly acquired physical measurements
and no completed engine/plugin/capture corpus in its original research package
(p. 2). Existing repository implementations have their own later evidence.

The sequence on p. 5 is M0 reference lock; M1 testable standalone core and offline
renderer; M2 shared generator; M3 console behavior; M4 scanner; M5 organ
electronics; M6 Leslie electronics; M7 rotary acoustics; M8 calibration; M9
product/release. Research waves, product milestones, and staffing waves are
different organizations. Pages 12, 16 and 18 require retained source, runtime,
audio, interface, persistence, and deployment evidence with explicit limits.
An attractive screenshot or a numerical pass cannot close those other gates.

## Uncertainties, errata and evidence not supplied

- The PDF references companion structured records; their existence and accuracy
  cannot be inferred from the PDF alone. The current repository's independently
  reconstructed tables must retain their own identities (pp. 2, 12, 36).
- No selected specimen serial, exact AO-28 applicability, calibrated contact
  traces, generator losses, complete physical gear inventory, source offsets,
  leakage matrix, driver impedance, or cabinet measurements are supplied by
  this reading (pp. 3, 17, 41-48, 65, 71, 77, 83-84).
- A roughly 9 mm white-key travel measurement is for one serviced 1967 C-3,
  not a universal B-3 tolerance (pp. 38, 42).
- The atlas warns of a one-based offset problem in a cited Hammondizer formula
  (p. 66): its explicit nominal reference is `440 * 2^((w-46)/12)` for tone
  `w=1..91`. Do not overwrite the existing verified registry with the cited
  paper's inconsistent offset.
- Page 72 rejects a cited claim that diameter alone explains a stated C-tone
  rotational speed; `f = tooth_count * rpm / 60` remains the consistency check.
- The 1965 filtering revision has separate applicability and possible retrofit;
  serial alone cannot settle the installed generator configuration (pp. 73-74).
- The atlas's initial numerical targets, for example p. 78's 0.1 dB/1 degree,
  are proposed comparison tolerances, not factory specifications or completed
  repository measurements. They cannot be rewritten to make a new run pass.
