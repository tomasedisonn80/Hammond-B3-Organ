## Consequences for the proposed M2 harmonic extension

The harmonic-source candidate is independently useful, but these downstream
chapters do not provide its harmonic amplitudes or phases. They reinforce the
following contract requirements before explicit opt-in integration:

1. **Name the output boundary.** Distinguish a normalized nominal source waveform,
   terminal voltage and flux linkage. Do not call arbitrary harmonic weights a
   calibrated pickup, and do not silently reinterpret normalized output as volts
   at the AO-28/Leslie interfaces. The later amplifier's gain and clipping depend
   on physical drive scale (pp. 255, 261, 265).
2. **Keep one phase owner for each shared source.** Harmonic phase must be derived
   from its source trajectory rather than restarted by notes, UI refresh or
   block boundaries. Preserve current source IDs, held-key sharing and rate-change
   semantics. Rotor histories remain distinct downstream owners (pp. 292,
   319–324).
3. **Predeclare bandwidth and headroom.** Extra generator harmonics change the
   bandwidth presented to contacts, nonlinear electronics and moving resampling.
   Admit or exclude terms through an explicit rate-aware policy, retain the
   chosen coefficients and document an output bound. Source band limitation is
   not proof of alias-free nonlinear or time-varying downstream processing
   (pp. 268, 303, 310, 330).
4. **Preserve phase and gain in comparisons.** Coherent sums, electrical polarity,
   driver paths and stereo interference depend on signed samples. Per-source or
   per-render automatic normalization can hide the amplitude changes that later
   drive electronics; do not introduce it as an undocumented convenience
   (pp. 263, 273, 280, 315).
5. **Keep provenance and software evidence distinct.** Synthetic spectra can
   qualify formula evaluation, lifecycle, allocation and reproducibility.
   Measured generator fidelity needs an identified capture set. Save model and
   coefficient identities with every audition; do not overwrite the existing
   `ideal-shared-sine-v1` baseline or historic evidence.
6. **Do not connect unfinished downstream work incidentally.** This phase can
   prepare and independently qualify harmonic output without adopting scanner,
   crossover or R4 acceptance. A future full graph must state conversion ports,
   loading assumptions, filters, latency and physical effect ownership explicitly.

Suggested bounded order is: freeze the prepared harmonic evaluator and coefficient
contract; build an independent scalar/spectral oracle; exercise source continuity,
all rates and rejection preservation; then review a separately identified opt-in
offline Engine/audition path. Preserve existing default output and state/parameter
compatibility before making it a playable default.
