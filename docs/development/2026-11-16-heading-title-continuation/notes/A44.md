the complete boundary suite exposed a necessary qualification: its maximum
resize case requires 256 tied resizes, both repeated and alternating sizes, to
pass within the original drain interval. Forcing a full paint after every
marker risks breaking that requirement. I immediately corrected the proposal
to root and A45 and explicitly retain the rejected alternative in the receipt.

The recommended main-only change retains eager pumping when `last_painted_`
exists and its workspace, presentation, and overlay equal the current UI.

next outer-loop pump. Do not compare dimensions, controls, pending state,
