## Observations and frozen proposal

The title's gross extent is already close, but its strokes look thinner and
flatter. I independently decoded both original images and reproduced every
authoring threshold count in the fixed `(590,14,490,46)` ROI. At encoded Y'>=100,
the reference has 5,415 pixels and the retained app has 3,863; their respective
half-open bounds are `(603,20,1067,53)` and `(602,20,1065,53)`. This predicate
has **no hue restriction** and is distinct from the C++ warm-ink geometry gate.
All five measured threshold pairs and source hashes are retained in the contract.

The proposal keeps every layout/fit calculation, size, face, original title
anchor, subtitle, rule and control. Five exact live-glyph passes add a dark lower
bevel, a half-font-unit upper highlight and one-font-unit horizontal weight,
while retaining the old cast shadow and a fixed main brass face. The contract
fixes colors, offsets and order before any candidate implementation or trial.
No new font, texture, global helper, additional clipping operation or mutable
musical state is introduced.

There are 15 characters and 14 nonempty glyphs: five calls require at most 70
copies versus 28 before, an increase of 42. This is a draw-call bound; SDL's
main-thread queue can allocate, so it proves neither allocation freedom nor
frame deadlines. The independent comparison must derive support from all old
and new glyph rectangles using binary32 placement, pinned SDL truncation,
physical origin and clip intersections. Broad title rectangles and masks
derived from candidate changes are excluded.
