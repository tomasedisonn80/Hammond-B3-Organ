### Whole-frame accounting, without a new similarity grade

At the unchanged 1672×941 origin, **1,385,301 of 1,573,352 RGB pixels differ**
between the original and current reference-size capture. Of the 188,051 equal
pixels, **185,002** belong to the exact cabinet rectangle
`(1132,174,466,397)`: that rectangle occupies **11.758462%** of the complete
window. Outside it, **3,049 of 1,388,350 pixels** happen to agree exactly.
The receipt's nine disjoint reporting bands account for every pixel once,
including outer furniture and margins. They are reporting partitions, not
painter allowances or permitted exclusions.

These raw counts demonstrate that crop equality cannot establish whole-window
equality. They are **not a perceptual quality score or newly failed material
gate**. The live scene includes different drawbar positions, held keys, correct
labels, extra functional inventory and a runtime footer. JPEG decoding and
different rasterization also affect exact comparison. All such differences
remain in the report; none becomes a waiver by being classified.

### A concrete next material distinction

On half-open patches `(x,y,w,h)`, decode RGB without resizing or color correction;
use encoded-channel `Y'=(2126R+7152G+722B)/10000`. Fit a plane to every pixel and
compute population RMS of the residual. These are image statistics, not physical
luminance or measured metal roughness. Values below are rounded for display;
the receipt retains exact rational sufficient statistics.

## Measurement rules to freeze before the next painter

1. **Alignment and complete extent.** Canonical comparison uses the complete
   1672×941 client canvas at the existing origin. Record logical size, physical
   output, renderer scale, viewport, clip, OS chrome exclusion and decoder.
   No fitted translation, perspective warp, per-panel registration, exposure/gain
   correction or best-looking crop may improve the result. A resized reference
   is a separately identified derivative, never the original exact-pixel target.
   Compare 1400×820 and other sizes against declared responsive geometry and
   their own genuine baseline; the cover supplies no independent native image
   at those dimensions.
2. **State and live evidence.** Retain the current real replay and its controls,
   desired/applied snapshots, held owners, focus, executable and production
   inventory. Use deterministic copied `UiModel`/`InstrumentViewInfo` scenes for
   exact before/after preservation, including fixed counters/meters. Actual app
   captures remain an additional product observation. A new reference-oriented
   registration scene must declare its native values before painting and remain
   additional to existing scenes; do not relabel different states as an exact
   old/new pair or replace live controls with artwork.
3. **Geometry versus visible edges.** Keep the existing exact canonical
   allocations and shared hit/paint endpoint tests, including their frozen
   0.002 logical-unit tolerance. Measure ink, lit faces, bevels and shadows
   separately. Reference brightness edges are uncertain visual landmarks;
   freeze sampled rows, edge definition and ambiguity before comparison, rather
   than choosing a convenient threshold for each candidate. A diffuse shadow
   is not a new hit boundary.
4. **Color and texture.** Preserve the original JPEG, its RGB identity and
   existing encoded-channel metrics. Freeze each new region's quiet patches,
   light-field samples, hue/channel statistics, residual texture distribution
   and spatial-periodicity observations before its candidate. No transfer of
   Upper tolerances to Lower, wood or ivory is justified by a shared palette.
   If a future color-managed metric is desired, give its transfer function and
   color-space conversion a new protocol identity and retain the original
   comparisons alongside it. Neither mean color nor texture RMS establishes
   material identity.
5. **Typography.** Separate same-string shape matching from required different
   text. For title, tabs and rack headings, record glyph ink bounds, cap height,
   baseline, tracking, stem width, highlight/shadow offset and clipped ink in
   fixed local boxes. Keep rasterizer/font identities and measured fitting.
   For corrected footages, state captions and device/status text, require every
   intended glyph, fitting and semantic meaning; the concept's wrong wording
   provides no glyph target. Inspect final composited contrast, not isolated
   text textures. Preserve full focus detail at small controls and existing
   readability requirements; shrinking type to improve an image score is not
   an admission strategy.
6. **Support and resources.** A new implementation slice needs a source-derived
   physical support manifest frozen independently before edits, with its own
   operations, float/raster rules, caller clip and device-pixel allowance.
   Existing Upper and whole-composition masks do not authorize it. Report exact
   exterior preservation and inactive equality, child changes, focus ink,
   renderer-state restoration and bounded work/storage. Keep full-frame review
   for seams, banding, sparkle, repeated grain and overdraw. A zero-exterior
   resource crop supplies no external-containment proof.
