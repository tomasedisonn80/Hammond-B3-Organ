## Remaining gaps and decision

The [updated gap record](../design/PRODUCT_REFERENCE_GAPS.md) retains exact
source links, inherited header measurements and the allocation table. A54's
review supports the following current findings:

- **Space:** reference-size racks remain higher and shorter; the upper manual
  starts about five pixels above the source landmark and the lower about seven
  below. These are retained approximate reference versus exact logical-layout
  comparisons, not new A54 measurements. Header size and cabinet column are
  already close; repeating the completed title-size work is not the next task.
- **Material:** separate dark wells, thin repetitive brass rims, regular key
  transitions and subdued lower wood rails still differ from the concept's
  broader bevels, recesses and varied light. A bounded deck/rim proposal is a
  later candidate, subject to usability findings and source ownership.
- **Type and contrast:** minimum preset, Service evidence, footer and modal
  helper text is small. Current fit, clipped-glyph and front-face checks do not
  establish comfortable native-display reading, standard contrast, target-size
  conformance or low-vision usability. No new contrast ratio was measured here.
- **Accessibility and devices:** visible keyboard focus is not semantic roles,
  values/actions, screen-reader exposure or a completed journey. Dummy captures
  and synchronous MIDI fixtures do not qualify physical display/audio/MIDI,
  hotplug/fault concurrency or actual device response.
- **Performance:** [A27](../development/2026-11-15-product-continuation/reports/A27-paint-performance.json)
  passes its six-case relative threshold, but wide software medians remain
  **112.441–113.713 ms**, p95 **127.045–131.616 ms**. That is not a 60-fps or
  input-latency result. Absolute display-response targets and measurements remain
  separate, as do audio callback deadlines.

These observations preserve the user's request and correctly accept only the
scoped software evidence. No new source edit or recapture is requested by this
review. Any defect discovered in the next live journey must first be coordinated
with root and the production/capture owners.
