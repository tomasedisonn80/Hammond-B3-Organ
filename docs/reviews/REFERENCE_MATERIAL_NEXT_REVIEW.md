## A52-M1 — Lower interpolation is an initialization, not a field specification

For the unchanged half-open patches, encoded-channel
`Y'=(2126R+7152G+722B)/10000` and plane-residual population RMS reproduce:

| Lower patch | Reference mean / RMS | Current mean / RMS |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `(612,320,18,128)` | 27.929753038194445 / 1.648025041753251 | 31.805590798611110 / 0.554379689237140 |
| `(1094,320,14,128)` | 20.590579464285714 / 1.450712070216063 | 32.013991852678570 / 0.983929468879251 |

The reference right-minus-left mean is **−7.339173573908731**; current is
**+0.208401054067460**. Independent exact RGB and Y′ coefficient solutions
agree with A41's fractions. Thus its stated bilinear arithmetic has support.
Neither Upper's positive lateral field nor its **2.0 / 0.4 / 1.5** tolerances
can be transferred to this different surface.

There is a specific limitation beyond the small sample count. At the two
strips' common vertical midpoint, the fitted bilinear field has the same
horizontal derivative everywhere, **−0.015289944945643 Y′ per pixel**.
The actual local-plane derivatives are **+0.067456761158411** on the left
and **−0.131003434065934** on the right. The fit uses the two means and two
vertical slopes; it does not constrain either horizontal slope. This is not
an arithmetic error, but shows why the field cannot be called a recovered
Lower illumination map. A grain adjustment could improve plane RMS while
leaving the low-frequency field wrong.

Five additional exploratory witnesses were chosen before their values were
computed. For example, `(612,460,18,20)` has reference mean
**24.713884444444446**, versus **22.692561800770996** from the initialization;
the left top witness differs in the opposite direction. Center-top
`(800,302,112,6)` is **28.588978273809524** versus **27.942025710941532**.
These are descriptive extrapolation checks, **not newly accepted quiet patches
or failed screens**. Heading/border influence and reference ambiguity must be
annotated before adopting them as targets. The receipt keeps all five results.

A future L1 contract must select and independently justify its actual sampling
domain, including hue/channel data, directional/row profiles and texture
distribution. It must then freeze coefficients as exact chosen constants,
binary32-to-binary64 transitions, operation order/FMA policy, channel rounding
and clamp, device-center coordinates, column resolution or equivalent sampling,
and behavior at clips. The proposed canonical pixel-center fractions do not
yet specify a resized raster algorithm. Grain requires its own count, seed,
mix/wrap arithmetic, positions/sizes, amplitude/opacity, collision order and
phase. Copying Upper's field, seed or primitive count would fill none of these
missing decisions.

## A52-T1 — Canonical arithmetic is correct; scaled glyph placement remains open

The actual [font atlas](../../apps/standalone/FontData.h),
[`centered`](../../apps/standalone/InstrumentView.cpp) and
[`Drawing::text`](../../apps/standalone/Drawing.cpp) reproduce ordinary footage
size/baseline **19 / 485.15** and mixed fractions **15 / 483.75** at canonical
size. All five selected same-string threshold-ink boxes/counts also reproduce;
the descriptive ink threshold does not measure complete glyph identity or
readability. Current mixed fractions visibly sit small/high in the full frames.

Under a sequential ASCII-component interpretation, A41's proposed four mixed
labels each occupy **40.9** logical advance units; `16'` occupies **31.2625**.
All nine correct strings remain representable, including `1 3/5'`. Nominal
glyph-quads fit below the rail and above the value row, but that arithmetic is
not an observed SDL raster result. The report records each component's nominal
baseline, origin, bearing and target rectangle without rendering a candidate.

**Resolve the scale rule before freezing.** The proposal says glyph sizes use
`f=min(sx,sy)` while horizontal positions/gaps use `sx`, and also requires width
to equal the sum of rendered advances. Those instructions need one explicit
cursor rule. With glyph advances scaled by `f` and two 0.5 gaps by `sx`, a mixed
run has width `39.9*f + sx`; it is not generally `40.9*sx`. At the admitted
1920×820 layout these are **35.917719620276** and **46.966507177033**. A canonical
origin table scaled by `sx` and a measured-advance cursor are different layouts.
The usual reference/minimum-active and nearly equal-aspect 1920×1080 examples
can hide this difference. Define baseline scaling, center, inter-component
positions, padded-width handling and fallback explicitly; include an active
case with `sx != sy` that exposes the chosen rule.

The software renderer's `SW_QueueCopy` truncates destination x/y/width/height;
unlike `SW_QueueFillRects`, it does **not** clamp copy extents to at least one.
`Drawing::text` also applies bearings, floating advances, per-dimension tiny
glyph gutters and texture modulation. An Upper grain-fill mask is not a glyph
mask. Freeze the union of the old/new actual glyph-copy quads with those rules,
including any offset or changed ruler primitive. Keep complete-string semantic
checks, per-glyph strong coverage and composited contrast; a count of bright
pixels cannot stand in for the slash, denominator or apostrophe being legible.

At 1400×820 the proposed 13-unit numerator/denominator is **10.885167464115**
nominal font pixels, with smaller ink. Actual display/DPI reading remains a
separate gate. Keep y491–506 numeric values, focus edges, all label/hit boxes,
pedal-mixture labeling and inactive paths intact. The straightforward proposed
run organization implies 62 nonempty glyph copies and 50 `Drawing::text` calls
over eighteen labels; these are nominal source counts, not measured timing or
SDL allocation claims. Freeze the chosen implementation's actual finite work.

T2's tracking and T3's title treatment need separate contracts. A52 also
reproduced the two heading ink boxes and title's Y′≥100 counts **5,415 / 3,863**.
These support further investigation, not a new font-weight tolerance or a
decision to replace the retained licensed atlas.

## Physical support, foreground, state and resource conditions

The actual Lower panel derives from mapped edges x600/x1116, y269/y508, then
the binary32 four-unit inset operation. At canonical scale the current gradient
is `[604,1112) × [273,504)`, **117,348 pixels**, and all **1,485 old grain pairs**
remain inside it. Its one-device-pixel expansion is
`[603,1113) × [272,505)`, **118,830 pixels**, agreeing with A41's arithmetic.
The receipt separately reconstructs six size/scale examples, including
fractional dimensions and unequal renderer scales. These establish **current
support only**. They cannot admit a future grain/curve/glyph operation that is
not yet specified, nor freeze a prospective padding allowance by coincidence.

Before each slice's first painter edit, independent ownership must freeze:

1. **Operation-derived support.** Enumerate old/new operations, actual float
   endpoints and SDL rules, then the declared device-pixel allowance and physical
   caller intersection. Never derive a mask from changed pixels or reuse the
   whole-composition/Upper allowance. Preserve all Upper v1.1 files/results;
   intentional later Upper typography is a new versioned child-paint transition,
   not an enlarged metal mask or a rewrite of its original preservation result.
2. **Foreground preservation.** For L1, retain shadows/borders/screws and all
   child arguments/order, layout/hit/copied-state identities, opaque well/cap
   interiors and exact cyan focus ink. Allow only justified compositing changes
   at unchanged translucent edges within the new support. Use Lower centers and
   Lower-focused 0/4/8 states; Upper's probes alone are insufficient. For T1,
   compare the intentionally changed glyphs against its new declared contract
   while preserving values, focus, surrounding material and all other text.
3. **Renderer state.** Preserve both scales, physical and logical viewport/clip
   observations, clip-enabled state and blend mode on all early/empty paths.
   Physical getters must be read at scale one; do not reinstall rounded getter
   values over fractional stored clips. Any helper that changes draw color
   directly must restore it; the whole-view painter does not promise global
   draw-color equality. Declare preservation for any newly touched texture
   modulation/filter/blend state rather than assuming a whole-view invariant.
4. **Finite matrix and work.** Assign explicit IDs/state tuples and coverage for
   reference/minimum-active/1920×1080, 1/1.5/2 scales, origin/(13,17), every changed
   edge's physical/fractional logical clips, enabled zero/disjoint clips, routes,
   pending/applied states, held owners and relevant focus. Preserve inactive
   threshold neighbors, compact, Flat, Rotary and Service exactly. Check clipped
   versus full companions, repeat/fresh renderer, and any unequal scale behavior
   actually claimed. Bound spans/marks/segments/copies, storage and maximum
   logical-size work before tuning; no image-sized or unbounded per-pixel noise
   assumption. Resource crops remain separate from ordinary full frames with a
   nonempty protected exterior. Source bounds prove no SDL allocation or deadline.

Require zero differing RGBA pixels/channels outside the new support and entire
equality for inactive/disjoint cases. Retain reference/baseline/candidate metrics,
all failures and spatial differences. Independently inspect complete reference
and minimum-active actual application frames for seams, banding, periodic grain,
sparkle, conspicuous edges and lost label/focus contrast. Those visual decisions
cannot be replaced by means/RMS or hashes.
