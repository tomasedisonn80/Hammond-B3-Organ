## Why these distinctions follow the project plan

[PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) separates reference/contracts, numerical implementation,
physical calibration and delivered-product qualification. The
[complete atlas reading](../research/CALIBRATION_ATLAS_REVIEW.md) belongs to the
six earlier actual readers. This assignment read that complete consolidation,
the project plan, standing rules, relevant contracts and extracted atlas pages
355, 364, 449 and 484–486. It does not claim another complete PDF reading or
independent verification of the atlas's external citations.

Those pages make the governing distinctions explicit: structure does not prove
a correct physical claim; raw observations and interpretations retain separate
identities; synchronization and declared sample rate do not prove clock
calibration; corrections cannot erase disagreement; tolerances and uncertainty
precede final evaluation; and changes retain old results while qualifying their
affected dependencies. A software pass, a playable adapter, a hosted build and
physical fidelity each need the evidence appropriate to their scope.
